## Project for review

ParishLink proposes a parishioner mobile app and an internal administration website for sacramental-service coordination and parish information management. The evaluation would use fictional cases for service discovery, appointments, document review/replacement, status/queue work and account recovery. Parish/clergy personnel retain business and canonical decisions.

Beneficiary confirmation, permission for a research pilot, individual participant consent and final system acceptance are separate decisions. Approval of one must not be recorded as approval of the others.

## Proposed measurements

Compare an approved replica of the manual process with the app using equivalent fictional cases, alternating order between participants and using separate practice cases. Record actual duration, independent/assisted completion, participant/system/operator/network errors, unexpected document return cycles and optional feedback. No improvement percentage, acceptance result or ISO quality score is currently claimed.

Participation must be voluntary, with withdrawal allowed without affecting services, eligibility or school standing. No real sacramental records or disclosure of participants' own service histories is needed. Review the [consent draft](CONSENT_DRAFT.md), [draft feedback instrument](DRAFT_FEEDBACK_INSTRUMENT.md) and [pilot protocol](../../docs/PILOT_PROTOCOL.md) before use.

## Limits to disclose

The system remains local; this work has not deployed it. Recovery code exists, but there is no email provider or sender domain. Both phones are available without test builds. Android push configuration exists; signed builds, device-accessible HTTPS API, physical acceptance, scheduler and iOS membership/APNs remain outstanding. Present these as unfinished acceptance work.

The immediate next step is beneficiary/workflow review while technical preparation continues. A stakeholder pilot starts only after the applicable written approvals and technical gates are satisfied.
