## Purpose

You are invited to help evaluate synthetic parish-service tasks using a manual workflow and ParishLink. The study measures task completion, elapsed time, errors, assistance, document rework, and feedback. It evaluates the workflow, not your personal ability.

## What participation involves

You will receive orientation and an unscored practice task, then complete only the role-appropriate approved tasks. Tasks use synthetic people, appointments, documents, and records. The planned duration, location, recording method, and compensation must be inserted in the approved version.

## Data collected

The study uses a pseudonymous participant code, coded role/parish/device/build/network details, task timestamps and outcomes, observed errors/assistance/rework, and approved feedback. Pilot files must not contain your name, contact information, account credentials, document numbers, or real sacramental information. Any audio/video/screen recording must be separately described and optional where required.

## Risks and safeguards

Possible risks include inconvenience, frustration, accidental disclosure, or discomfort with observation. You may pause or stop. Real records are prohibited; test accounts and synthetic cases are used. Access, storage, retention, deletion, breach response, and who may view data must be stated in the approved version.

## Voluntary participation

Participation is voluntary. Choosing not to participate or withdrawing will not affect parish services, sacramental eligibility, or your relationship with the parish/institution. The approved version must explain whether already collected data can be removed after withdrawal and any limit on doing so.

## Consent record

The approved consent process records consent outside the public repository. A blank or draft status is not consent. Do not sign this draft.
