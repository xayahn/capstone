# Pilot Coordinator Briefing

**Status:** Draft for approval. Do not deliver to participants until authorization, instrument, and consent gates are complete.

## Before each session

- Confirm the approved protocol/instrument/consent versions, participant adult eligibility, role match, and counterbalance assignment.
- Prepare only synthetic cases, approved manual replicas, test accounts, devices, build, network profile, timers, and reset steps.
- Verify no real parishioner or sacramental information is visible.
- Assign pseudonymous participant, observer, device, and parish codes.
- Confirm withdrawal, incident, accessibility, and technical-stop procedures.

## Neutral opening script

“We are evaluating the workflow, not you. Some tasks may be difficult because we need to discover what should be improved. You may stop or withdraw according to the consent information. Please do not enter real personal, sacramental, document, account, or parishioner information. Think aloud only if the approved protocol asks you to. During scored tasks I may repeat the instruction, but other help will be recorded as assistance.”

## Observation discipline

- Start/stop the timer using the protocol definitions.
- Do not teach during scored tasks. Repeat task wording neutrally; use only the approved rescue rule.
- Record observable errors, assistance, rework, pauses, and environment incidents at the time they occur.
- Do not infer motivation, competence, disability, or identity in notes.
- Stop for privacy/security exposure, consent uncertainty, distress, or invalid environment.

## Closeout

- Administer only the approved feedback instrument.
- Remind participants not to place identifying information in comments.
- Debrief, provide approved contact/withdrawal information, revoke test access, reset synthetic state, and transfer evidence to approved storage.
- Record deviations without rewriting original observations.
